Common questions

Stride shows no activities. Open the Health app, tap your profile picture, then Apps → Stride, and turn on the data types you want Stride to use. Then pull down to refresh in Stride.

Where are my Garmin workouts? Stride imports the activities CSV from Garmin Connect on the web: Activities → All Activities → Export CSV. Put the file on your iPhone and choose Settings → Garmin → Import CSV. The export doesn't include GPS routes or daily steps.

The Apple Watch app shows no bests. Open Stride on your iPhone once; it sends your personal bests to the watch automatically.

How do I add a widget? Touch and hold the Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Stride. Today vs Best and All Metrics are free; Personal Bests and Leaderboard come with Stride Supporter.

What does Stride Supporter do? It's an optional one-off purchase that supports development and unlocks the Personal Bests and Leaderboard widgets, the Apple Watch face complications and six alternate app icons (Settings → Support Stride). Everything else is free. Bought it on another device or reinstalled? Tap Restore Purchase on the same screen.

My watch complication still shows a lock. Open Stride on your iPhone with your watch nearby; it tells the watch you're a supporter.

Requesting a refund. Purchases are handled by Apple: visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy
