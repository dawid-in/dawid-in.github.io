Challenge Track

Your monthly activity challenge, at a glance

Every month the Fitness app sets you a personal activity challenge. Enter it once and Challenge Track works out your progress from Apple Health, on your iPhone, widgets and Apple Watch.

  • What's left, days remaining and how many days you can still miss
  • Daily amounts, monthly totals, daily averages, ring goals and workout counts
  • Reminders and a history of past months with your streak
  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, watch app and complications
