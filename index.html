Challenge Track

Your monthly activity challenge, at a glance

Every month the Fitness app sets you a personal activity challenge. Enter it once and Challenge Track works out your progress from Apple Health, on your iPhone, widgets and Apple Watch.

  • Progress ring for the month, with today inside it, plus what's left and days you can still miss
  • Daily amounts, monthly totals, daily averages, ring goals and workout counts
  • Day by Day chart, reminders and a history of past months with your streak
  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, watch app and complications

Stride

Personal leaderboards for every walk, run and workout

Stride ranks your activity from Apple Health: your longest runs, fastest paces, biggest days and today against your best.

  • Top 10 and Top 100 workouts by distance, duration, calories or pace, with place and route map
  • Daily steps, calories, distance and exercise against your best day ever
  • Apple Watch app and Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets
  • Optional Garmin Connect CSV import, merged with your Health workouts
