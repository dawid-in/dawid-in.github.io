Challenge Track Privacy Policy

Effective 27 October 2026

Challenge Track is an iPhone and Apple Watch app, published by Dawid Pachla, that shows your progress toward a monthly activity challenge. In short: the app has no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. Your data stays on your own devices and in your own iCloud, and the developer never receives it.

Health data

With your permission, Challenge Track reads the following from Apple Health: your activity rings and their goals, active energy, exercise minutes, stand hours, walking and running distance, and workouts. It uses them only to work out your progress toward the challenge you entered. The app reads Health data and never writes to it.

Health data is processed on your device. It is not sent to the developer, and it is not used for advertising, marketing or data mining, or sold or shared with third parties.

You can change or withdraw this permission at any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Challenge Track.

What the app stores

The app stores on your device the challenge you entered, your progress, the amount recorded for each day of the challenge month, your history of past challenges, your reminder settings and widget settings, the app icon you chose, whether you have made the supporter purchase, and the date you first opened the app, which is used to time an occasional suggestion to support the developer. This is kept in the app's own storage, where the app's widgets can read it.

If you use the Apple Watch app, your iPhone sends the challenge and your latest progress directly to your own paired watch.

Your history of past challenges, which includes each month's result and the amount recorded for each day of that month, is also saved to your own iCloud account, so that it survives a new iPhone or reinstalling the app. It is stored by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, the developer has no access to it, and you can turn it off for Challenge Track in Settings → your name → iCloud.

If you back up your iPhone or Apple Watch, for example with iCloud Backup, the app's stored data may be included in that backup. Backups are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and the developer has no access to them.

Purchases

Challenge Track is free. It offers an optional one-time purchase to support the developer, which adds the Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, a choice of app icons and colours, more detailed charts, and trends across your past months. The trends are worked out on your device from the history described above. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store, and the app checks whether you have made the purchase with the App Store on your device. The developer does not receive your name, Apple Account, payment details or any information that identifies you; Apple provides only overall sales figures.

Ratings

The app may ask you to rate it using Apple's standard rating prompt. Apple shows that prompt and handles any rating or review you leave; the app does not receive it.

Notifications

Reminders are optional and are created on your device. No notification is sent from a server.

Tracking and third parties

The app does not track you, contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or other third-party code, and does not use cookies.

Deleting your data

You can remove the current challenge in the app, which also removes it from your history. Deleting the app from your iPhone and Apple Watch removes everything it has stored on them. The copy of your history in iCloud can be deleted in Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage. Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing for the developer to delete.

Children

Challenge Track is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy
